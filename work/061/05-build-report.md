## Deviations from plan
1. `_pick` signature: the plan's is `_pick(g, today, cfg, calendar, basis)`. The built one is
   `_pick(g, today, rule, cfg, monthly, basis)`:
   - Both the realtest rule and the bot config dict are needed.
   - It takes `monthly = third_fridays(calendar)` and not the calendar itself. Recomputing it on every call (~1.7M calls
     per run) would add minutes.
2. Small fail-closed additions in the spirit of 1.8 that the plan does not list:
   - Basis is split-adjusted with the strikes (CMCSA, CSX had splits). Without it the floor would sit at 2× after a 2-for-1.
   - A non-finite or non-positive split factor is a ValueError, where it used to be skipped silently.
   - A non-finite or non-positive close is a ValueError.
   - A missing `asset_type` in tickers.json is treated as a stock, with a warning (`REALTEST_MISSING["asset_type"]`,
     plus a drop case).
   - None of these fire on the committed data (17 tickers load, same skipped set).
3. EARNINGS_UNKNOWN applies only to `skip_earnings=True` rules. The grid's hold-through-earnings ("E") variants don't
   use earnings, so they are not blocked when dates are unknown. The bot rule is a skip-earnings rule.
4. Entry check order: EARNINGS_UNKNOWN comes before the expiry pick, EARNINGS_BLACKOUT after the strike pick. In the bot,
   blackout comes before strike. Counts can shift between NO_STRIKE_IN_TOLERANCE / BASIS_ABOVE_BAND and
   EARNINGS_BLACKOUT; whether a call is sold does not change.
5. `fits_3k` uses the last close (`lot_cost_last`). Both `lot_cost_first` and `lot_cost_last` are reported.
6. The 03 Missing-inputs rows are not edited. The plan says the volhist row is superseded (NaN + `atm_na_reason`). The
   BXM text is now "BXM_History.csv: not supplied" and the KGC reason is "no option quotes". I3 should update 03 or
   accept this note.
7. Files outside the plan's 4 paths: `work/061/03-handoff.md`, as the coordinator asked (fact-check rows and Totals).
8. Gap found, not fixed (the plan's 120-day check doesn't cover it). For I1/I3: BAC, CSX and USB have no earnings date
   before April 2020, so their January 2020 report is missing. Each sold a 2020-01-06 call expiring 2020-02-21 that may
   have been held through it. A check on the gap between the first snapshot and the first earnings date would catch it.
   01 lists it under approximate numbers.
9. 01's proposed rules 1, 2, 4 and 4b no longer have support from the re-run (the grid spread is under 0.5 pt). 01 keeps
   them with their new numbers and a note, and adds item 8 (the cost-basis floor is the main driver) for the person.
   This is a research judgement for I3 to check.
