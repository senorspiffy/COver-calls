## 3 ways FOR
1. **Make step 1 a screen result, not a ticker class (R1's wording).** This closes the bug: the rule can no longer name an empty class. It also states what the bot already does (`feasible_universe` + UNIVERSE_EMPTY). Reasoning: 002/03 L114 shows the class "diversified ETF ≤ $28" is empty, while the screen is computed every scan.
2. **Drop "5 of the last 10 scans" as a gate. Keep it as a logged count. This is simpler than R1's proposal and lets the person act sooner.** The order-time screen (spread ≤ $0.05, OI ≥ 500, in-tolerance strike) already blocks an illiquid trade on the day it would happen. The history rule only protects against one-day flukes. What waiting costs: 10 scans is about 2 trading weeks, roughly half a monthly cycle. With 001's example premium of $46.90 a month, that is about $23. The rule protects against at most one spread: $0.05 × 100 = $5 a side at the screen's limit. So print `passed n of last k scans` in the daily report as information, and gate only on today's screen. If the person still wants a history gate, use **3 of the last 5** with `HISTORY_SHORT` as a warning in paper mode and a block only in a future live mode. Reasoning only; no external source.
3. **Feasibility field in `templates/research-handoff.md` + R3 BLOCKED check (request guardrail 1).** It hits the RI-001 root cause where it arises (R3 → I1). The Nikkei 225 covered-call index (Japanese row below) avoids the problem by construction: it always sells "直近限月" (the nearest month). A rule that picks "the nearest X" instead of filtering to "a window" has a candidate on every day.

## 3 ways AGAINST
1. **R1's "block until 10 logged scans" is too restrictive for someone who wants action, and it adds a new unknown→block path to test.** On day 1 every ticker is `HISTORY_SHORT`, so the bot cannot trade for about 2 weeks even when today's quote passes. That is defensible under RI-002 ("unknown blocks"), but the count is not a safety input: today's screen is. Reasoning (FOR 2).
2. **Even the corrected rule may be empty at $3k today.** Its monthly (30–45 DTE / nearest-35) feasibility is UNVERIFIED for F, SOFI, KWEB and T, because only weeklies were read (003/01 F4). The new feasibility field would honestly mark step 1 "0 confirmed" right now. The person should hear plainly: the real-ticker universe at $3k is unproven until the paper bot logs monthly quotes.
3. **The template field can become box-ticking.** R3 copied "always found" in 002 without computing it, and a field does not force a computation. It needs a command or test name next to the number, so R3/I3 can re-run it (A1's 012 "counts by command" idea). Reasoning from the RI-001 history.
