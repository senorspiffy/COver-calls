## Correction to 001 (for A1: open a BUG item)
**Error:** 001 step 1 says "$3,000 is enough to start with one position, but only on a diversified, liquid ETF priced ≤ $28". The arithmetic is right: ($3,000 − $200) / 100 = $28. But the rule assumes such an ETF exists, and nobody ran the screen. On 2026-10-30, R1/R2 and this check found **no diversified ETF ≤ $28 with liquid options**. The four ≤ $28 ETFs that pass the liquidity screen (KWEB, BITO, UNG, MSOS) are concentrated, and they pass only at far-OTM strikes outside 001's 0.25–0.30Δ band. As written, step 1 gives an empty universe at $3k.

**Corrected rule (replaces 001 step 1, second sentence):** "At $3,000, trade one underlying priced ≤ $28 (100 sh ≤ cash − $200) **that passes the step-3 screen at the 0.25–0.30Δ strike of the 30–45 DTE monthly** on at least 5 of the last 10 logged scans. Prefer a diversified ETF if one passes. As of 2026-10-30 none does. If no ticker passes, do not force a trade. Instead: (a) sell a cash-secured put on a ticker that passes, subject to the 50% single-name cap; (b) wait or add funds; or (c) hold a covered-call ETF (001 alt. 2). Single stocks are capped at 50% of capital (001 step 9a). So at $3k a single stock is only allowed if 100 shares cost ≤ $1,500, or as a CSP sized within the cap."

**Proposed guardrail (A1 may improve):**
1. *Process:* any rule that restricts the trading universe (asset type, price cap, liquidity) must cite a **dated screen run that shows at least one passing ticker**. R3 rejects a rule without one.
2. *Code (built in this item, step 12):* `screen.feasible_universe(config, snapshots)` runs on every bot scan and backtest. If it returns empty, the report shows a blocking **"UNIVERSE EMPTY: rule X has no passing ticker"** banner and no trade is made. A unit test fails if the shipped config's screen has no passing ticker on the bundled sample snapshot.
